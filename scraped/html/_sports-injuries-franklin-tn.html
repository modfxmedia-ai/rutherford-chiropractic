Franklin Sports Injuries

Get Back in the Game: Sports Injury Recovery in Franklin

That nagging pain or sudden strain from your weekend game is slowing you down. Here in Franklin, we help athletes recover naturally and get back to peak performance. Visit us at our Murfreesboro office—just a short drive for dedicated Franklin athletes.

=== INTRO ===

You’ve put in the work on the field, court, or trail. But now, an injury has you sidelined, and it’s more than just physical—it’s frustrating. You want to heal properly, not just mask the pain with pills or rush back before you’re ready. That’s where we come in.

At Rutherford Spine and Wellness Center, we specialize in helping Franklin athletes recover from sports injuries. Whether it’s a nagging sprain, a recurring back issue from golf, or a strain from a hard workout, we focus on getting to the root cause of your pain. Our team understands the demands of being active in our community, and we’re here to help you move freely again.

We use a patient-focused approach to relieve pain, restore function, and improve your overall performance. When you’re ready to stop letting an injury hold you back, call (615) 217-0097 to schedule your visit.

=== WHY_CHOOSE_US ===

Why Franklin Athletes Choose Our Recovery Method

  • We Focus on Root Causes, Not Just Symptoms – Instead of just treating the sore spot, we look at how your entire body is moving to find the source of your pain and prevent it from returning.
  • Personalized Recovery Plans – No two injuries are the same. We build a recovery strategy tailored to your sport, your goals, and your body’s specific needs.
  • Active Recovery Techniques – We combine hands-on adjustments with targeted exercises and stretches to restore mobility and strengthen the surrounding muscles.
  • Experienced Team – Our trained professionals have worked with athletes of all levels and understand the biomechanics of sports-related injuries.
  • Transparent Process – You’ll know exactly what to expect at every step, with clear explanations and no pressure to commit to unnecessary treatments.

=== PROCESS ===

From Your First Visit to Full Recovery

  1. Schedule Your Evaluation – Call (615) 217-0097 to book your initial appointment. We’ll discuss your injury history, your activity level, and your recovery goals.
  2. Comprehensive Assessment – During your first visit, we’ll perform a detailed examination to understand your injury and how it’s affecting your movement and performance.
  3. Your Custom Recovery Plan – Based on our findings, we’ll create a clear plan that may include adjustments, soft tissue work, and specific exercises to help you heal.
  4. Ongoing Support & Progress – As you progress, we’ll adjust your plan as needed to ensure you’re healing properly and safely returning to the activities you love.

=== FAQ ===

Quick Answers to Common Questions

Do I need a referral to see you for a sports injury?

No referral is needed. You can call us directly to schedule your evaluation, and we’ll take it from there.

How long does it take to recover from a sports injury?

Recovery time varies depending on the injury, its severity, and how consistently you follow your plan. Contact us to discuss your timeline so we can give you a realistic expectation.

What should I expect during my first appointment?

We’ll discuss your injury and goals, perform a thorough assessment, and explain our findings. You’ll leave with a clear understanding of your recovery path.

Do you accept insurance?

We accept a variety of insurance plans. For specific coverage details and to discuss payment options, please contact our office at (615) 217-0097.
